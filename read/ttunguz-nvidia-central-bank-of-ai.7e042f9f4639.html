英伟达宣布黄仁勋辞去 CEO 的当天，Tomasz Tunguz 发了一篇 X Article。他把过去四年的英伟达比作 “AI 的中央银行”，黄仁勋就是这家央行的行长。现在临时 CEO 由 CFO Colette Kress 接任，董事长由 Harvey Jones 出任。他要回答的问题是：行长换人，AI 经济会怎样？

他给出了五条推论。

英伟达这两年放出去多少钱

第一条用的是一组数字。按他的统计，过去两年英伟达：

  • 投入 420 亿美元做并购和技术收购；
  • 以成本计投入 610 亿美元现金，持有 229 家初创公司和 13 家上市公司的股权；
  • 提供了 1645 亿美元的租约担保和云算力兜底承诺，这些承诺需要配套的资本储备；
  • 账上还有 631 亿美元客户应收账款，账期现在最长拉到一整年。

他的类比是美联储：央行收紧信贷，整体经济就放缓。换成 CFO 主导、又面对董事会更严格审视的英伟达，只要在股权投资、付款条件或租约兜底中任何一项上收紧，AI 基建就会跟着放慢。

一个人撑起来的生态

第二条，他认为英伟达把初创公司和芯片两个生态绑在一起的方式前所未见。在黄仁勋手下，英伟达对 200 多家初创公司同时扮演四个角色：芯片供应商、领投方、云算力的锚定客户、租约担保人。

这些交易靠的是黄仁勋个人的判断和关系。接下来英伟达需要一位领导者，在没有这位联合创始人的情况下，把这些和创始人之间的合作关系维持下去。

开源模型公司少了最大的金主

第三条，按他的说法，英伟达是美国开源 AI 公司最大的买家和出资方，向 Nemotron 体系投入了 220 亿美元，覆盖 Hugging Face、Poolside 和 Essential AI。他说没有第二家公司在这个规模上收编开源团队。

Meta、Google，以及至少八家融资超过 1 亿美元的美国初创公司还在训练开放权重模型。今后美国开源的担子，要更多落在它们自己身上。

出口管制收紧，定制芯片得到窗口

第四条比较简短：他判断这件事大概率会加大华盛顿收紧半导体出口管制和合规规则的压力。

第五条是定制芯片的机会。他给出的数据是，2026 年定制 AI ASIC 已占 AI 服务器出货量的 28%、加速器收入的 15% 到 20%。其中 Google TPU 占定制芯片市场的 65%，其余玩家包括 AWS 的 Trainium3、Meta 的 MTIA 和 Broadcom 的定制 XPU。单一来源的供应商高层出现动荡，会加快客户向定制芯片转移。

接下来盯什么

Tunguz 的结论是，英伟达在创业融资、并购退出和产业政策上的中心地位怎么强调都不为过；最高层突然换人，给市场尤其是初创公司带来了实实在在的不确定性。

想检验他的判断，可以盯三个信号：英伟达新的股权投资和租约兜底有没有放缓；客户应收账款的账期有没有收短；定制 ASIC 在 AI 服务器出货中的占比能不能继续从 28% 往上走。文中金额与比例都来自作者的统计，本站未逐项复核。原文同时发布在 tomtunguz.com。

When the Central Bank of AI Changes Chairs

For the past four years, Jensen Huang ran the Central Bank of AI. Today he resigned as chief executive of Nvidia, handing the interim reins to chief financial officer Colette Kress & the chairmanship to Harvey Jones. What are the implications for the AI economy?

  1. If the Central Bank of AI tightens policy, the AI economy slows. Over the past two years, Nvidia committed $42b to M&A & technology buyouts, invested $61b of cash on a cost basis into equity stakes across 229 startups & 13 public companies, & extended $164.5b in lease guarantees & cloud capacity backstops that require capital reserves alongside $63.1b in customer receivables, where payment terms now stretch up to a full year. Under a CFO-led regime facing heightened board scrutiny, any tightening of those equity checks, payment terms or lease backstops will slow AI buildouts just as the broader economy slows when the Federal Reserve tightens credit.
  2. Nvidia fused the startup & chip ecosystems in a way we have never seen. Under Huang, Nvidia acted simultaneously as chip supplier, lead venture investor, anchor cloud customer & lease guarantor across more than 200 startups. Those deals ran on Huang's personal conviction & relationships. Nvidia now needs a leader who can sustain these founder partnerships without the co-founder who built them.
  3. Open-source startups lose their primary patron & buyer. Nvidia has been the single largest buyer & financier of American open-source AI companies, committing $22b to its Nemotron stack across Hugging Face, Poolside & Essential AI : no other company has bought open-source teams at scale. Meta, Google & at least eight US startups that have raised $100m or more continue to train open-weight models, & they will now have to carry more of the American open-source mantle themselves.
  4. Tighter export controls. The episode likely increases pressure in Washington for stricter semiconductor export controls & compliance rules.
  5. An opening for custom silicon. Custom AI ASICs already account for 28% of AI server shipments & 15% to 20% of accelerator revenue in 2026, led by Google's TPUs at 65% of the custom silicon market alongside AWS's Trainium3, Meta's MTIA & Broadcom's custom XPUs. Leadership turbulence at a single-source supplier accelerates that shift. The change of leadership at the Central Bank of AI is a pivotal moment for the industry. It is hard to overstate how central Nvidia has become to startup financing, M&A exits & policy across the fastest-growing segment of the US economy. A sudden transition at the top introduces real uncertainty into the market & especially in startups. Originally published on tomtunguz.com.
