Simon Taylor：黄仁勋离任，英伟达撑起的 AI“循环融资”会怎样

英伟达同时是卖家、股东和担保人。他认为新支持会暂停，GPU 抵押贷款会变贵，兜底角色会慢慢转给 OpenAI 这类算力用户。

英伟达为 OpenAI 在俄亥俄州的一个园区承诺了最高 1050 亿美元的担保。Simon Taylor 写这条帖子时，按他的说法，扮演“美联储主席”的黄仁勋刚刚离任，英伟达股价开盘跌了 14%。他想算清楚的是：AI 行业的“循环融资”接下来会怎样。

英伟达是 AI 的最后贷款人

他的定位很直接：英伟达是 AI 的最后贷款人，现在它自己也在经历信心危机。

英伟达卖芯片，同时持有买芯片公司的股份，还为其中一些公司的租约做担保。他举的例子是，英伟达 1 月向 CoreWeave 投了 20 亿美元，并同意买下最多 63 亿美元 CoreWeave 卖不出去的算力。

这些合同把英伟达和客户绑在一起。以俄亥俄园区为例，英伟达只有在 OpenAI 违约时才需要付钱，而且只付房东把场地重新出租或出售之后仍然缺的那一部分。

拍板的人走了

他强调，这些决定是黄仁勋做的。他引用 D.A. Davidson 分析师 Gil Luria 的说法：支持 CoreWeave 和 Nebius 的决定主要出自黄仁勋。10 月，信用市场给出的 CoreWeave 五年违约概率一度超过 60%，黄仁勋上 Bloomberg 表示 CoreWeave 不需要英伟达救助。

Kress 参与搭建了这套融资计划，现在以临时 CEO 的身份执掌它，同时还要应对国会对芯片出口的问询。他判断，在正式 CEO 到位之前，英伟达不太可能再给陷入困境的客户提供新的支持。

贷款人已经冲进来了

另一边，债权人的敞口已经很大。他引用 American Compute 的数据：今年截至 10 月 10 日，GPU 抵押贷款一共放出了 484 亿美元，而 2025 年全年只有 66 亿美元。

CoreWeave 本周表示，在当前收益率下，2027 年之前不会进入无担保债券市场，资金来源改为客户预付款和以芯片作抵押的贷款。

有收入就还能融资，只是更慢

他并不悲观。只要有收入，就还能融到钱，只是速度可能没那么快。

  • 有大客户预付款的 neocloud，仍然能融资；
  • 原本要靠英伟达站在贷款背后的那些，要么付更高的利息，要么把算力卖给别人。

他预计，随着 OpenAI 这类算力使用方走向盈利，兜底的角色会从英伟达逐渐转到它们身上。

他怎么看“我早说过”

他预料 Ed Zitron 等 AI 看空者会说“我早就说过”。他拿 FTX 倒下作比：那之后加密行业确实沉寂了一阵，但随后迎来了稳定币的热潮和真实的商业场景。他的结论是，这是“开端的结束”，还谈不上“终结的开始”。

对关注 AI 基建融资的人，这条帖子指了几个可以跟踪的变量：英伟达对 CoreWeave、Nebius 这类客户的新增支持是否暂停；GPU 抵押贷款的利率和发放速度；以及 neocloud 的客户预付款能否接替英伟达的兜底。文中金额和比例为作者引述，本站未逐项核实。

Jensen Huang just resigned as Nvidia CEO after 33 years.

Jensen Huang just resigned as Nvidia CEO after 33 years.

Just when you think things can't get crazier, the world does this.

What does it mean for "circular finance"

For example: Nvidia has promised up to $105 billion in guarantees for a single OpenAI campus in Ohio. But it's "Fed Chair" Jensen has gone and its stock fell 14% at open.

How do those two things compute? Would need a heck of a GPU for that!

Nvidia is the lender of last resort for AI, going through its own confidence crisis.

Nvidia sells the chips, owns stakes in the companies that buy them, and guarantees some of their leases. It put $2B into CoreWeave in January and agreed to buy up to $6.3B of its unsold capacity.

Those contracts bind Nvidia to its customer. In Ohio, Nvidia pays only if OpenAI defaults, and then only the shortfall after the landlord re-lets or sells the site.

Jensen was making these calls.

D.A. Davidson analyst Gil Luria says the decisions to back CoreWeave and Nebius were largely Jensen's. In October, when credit markets put CoreWeave's five-year default risk above 60%, Jensen went on Bloomberg and said it needed no rescue from Nvidia.

Kress helped build the financing programme and now runs it as interim CEO, with Congress asking about chip exports. New support for a struggling customer looks unlikely until a permanent CEO arrives.

Lenders pilled in and wrote $48.4B of GPU-backed loans this year to 10 October, up from $6.6B in all of 2025 (American Compute).

CoreWeave said this week it will stay out of the unsecured bond market through 2027 at current yields, and fund itself with customer prepayments and loans secured on chips.

But look, where there's revenue there's still the ability to raise. Just maybe not at the same speed.

A neocloud with a large customer prepaying can still raise. One that needed Nvidia behind its loan will pay more, or sell its capacity to someone else.

Over time, the backstop moves from Nvidia to the companies that use them like OpenAI, as they reach profitability

Cue every single AI bear (Zitron et al) saying I told you so...

This is like when FTX went down. Sure, crypto went quiet for a while.

But then we had stablecoin summer and real business cases.

This isn't the beginning of the end; it's the end of the beginning.
