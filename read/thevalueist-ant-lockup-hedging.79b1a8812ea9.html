中文改写

Anthropic（股票代码 ANT）上市首日，赶上了今年 AI 板块最弱的一天，股价从开盘起就承压。最省事的解读是市场不认这个估值。TheValueist 认为有一个更贴切的机械性解释，得从今天之前谁持有这家公司说起。

锁着的暴利，最自然的操作是对冲

他写道，Anthropic 的股东名册里不只有机构。多年的私募融资、老股转让和 SPV（特殊目的载体）积累下一长串私人个人投资者，手里是上市前的股票，很多人账面上赚了 10 到 50 倍。从今天上午起，这些账面收益按市价计值，但大部分卖不掉，锁定期最长一整年。

处在这个位置的持有人，有一个现成的交易：拿锁定的股票做底，去卖空上市交易的股票，或者等期权上市后买看跌期权。这样一来，账面暴利就变成锁定的收益，不管接下来一年怎么走。股价从这里每跌一点，对冲端就赚一点，而对应的股票要到 2027 年才能卖。

三重摩擦，所以是压力而非洪水

他列了三个障碍，用来解释为什么这只是持续的压力，不会变成抛售洪水：

  1. 锁定协议禁止对冲到第 195 天，也就是 2027 年 6 月 1 日，之后也只允许对冲持有人剩余受限股的 20%；
  2. 对箱卖空（short against the box）在税务上算推定出售，收益要像已经实现一样缴税；
  3. 流通盘只有约 7%，可借的股票稀缺、昂贵，而且随时可能被召回。

但“被发现”不在障碍之列。卖空数据每月公布两次，只是一个不带名字的汇总数。没有任何申报文件会显示某个小股东的空头。公司和承销商作为锁定协议的签约方，看不到任何人的券商账户。对于成千上万的小型私人持有人，他的判断是：没有人会知道。

所以他认为，这只股票早期的空头头寸里，有一部分可能来自持有人给自己卖不掉的暴利买保险，下注者未必看空 Anthropic。他建议盯三个痕迹：12 月中旬公布的第一份卖空数据、ANT 期权上市后的看跌偏度，以及从 12 月 15 日开始的各个解禁日，这些日期会检验那些没法对冲的持有人到底卖出了多少可卖的股票。

这种动机之所以会持续，原因在解禁时间表本身。下面是他整理的完整版本。

制造动机的锁定安排

Anthropic 定价 83 美元，公众手里约 9.8 亿股，大约占 130.9 亿股总股本的 7%。流通盘背后有约 155 亿股受限股、期权和奖励股，它们不会在同一天解禁。招股书设了一整年内 23 个解禁日，部分按固定天数，部分挂钩财报发布。下面每个数字都只是可卖上限，招股书没有说任何持有人打算卖出。

两条锁定轨道

  • 180 天轨道：约 27 亿股，持有人是员工、非创始人董事和高管，以及另一组股东。锁定最长 180 天，在 2027 年一季度财报发布后第二个交易日与 2027 年 5 月 17 日之间，以较早者结束。
  • 365 天轨道：约 80 亿股，持有人是七位联合创始人和持股超过 0.5% 的大股东。锁定最长 365 天，在 2027 年三季度财报发布后第二个交易日与 2027 年 11 月 18 日之间，以较早者结束。

解禁日历

  • 2026 年 11 月 19 日（上市首日）：最多 3.16 亿股，是非创始人内部人士 10% 的额度。招股书点名首席财务官和首席会计官从第一个交易日起就有资格出售。
  • 2026 年 12 月 15 日和 2027 年 1 月 5 日：每次最多 2.99 亿股，累计 9.14 亿股。
  • 2027 年 1 月 19 日和 2 月 2 日：每次最多 3.49 亿股，累计 16.1 亿股，大约是招股书所列发行股数的两倍。
  • 2026 财年财报发布后第二个交易日：最多 17 亿股，累计 33.1 亿股。内部人士的另外 35% 额度在这一步释放。
  • 2026 财年财报发布后第 13、27、41、55、69 天：每次最多 4.74 亿股，累计 56.8 亿股。
  • 2027 年一季度财报发布或 5 月 17 日，以较早者为准：最多 30 亿股，累计 86.8 亿股。这是整个结构里最大的单次解禁，180 天轨道在此结束。
  • 一季度财报发布后第 34、48、62、76 天：每次最多 4.49 亿股，累计 104.8 亿股。
  • 2027 年二季度财报发布后第二个交易日：最多 5.62 亿股，累计 110.4 亿股。
  • 二季度财报发布后第 27、41、55、69、83 天：每次最多 5.05 亿股，累计 135.7 亿股。
  • 2027 年三季度财报发布或 11 月 18 日，以较早者为准：剩余 19 亿股，累计 154.7 亿股，全部解禁。

365 天轨道内部的比例

对大股东来说，解禁比例依次是：第 27 天和第 48 天各 1.8%；第 62 天和第 76 天各 2.1%；2026 财年财报后 3.75%；之后五次各 2.85%；180 天节点 4.2%，累计 30.0%；一季度财报后四次各 5.6%；二季度财报后 7.0%；之后五次各 6.3%，累计 90.9%；最后释放余额。

换成具体股数：持有 1 亿股的股东，到 2 月 2 日能卖 780 万股，到明年 5 月能卖 3000 万股，其余要再等一年。

侧门

  • 对冲：禁止到第 195 天，即 2027 年 6 月 1 日；之后部分股东可以对冲剩余受限股的最多 20%，合计约 7.19 亿股。承销商也可以自行决定提前释放股份，而且某些释放会触发其他持有人的对等释放。
  • N 类股：与 IPO 同时出售给谷歌、英伟达、AMD、博通、美光、微软和 TSMC Partners 的 260 亿美元 N 类股，完全没有锁定期。它属于限制性股票，非关联方通常六个月内不能转售。
  • 员工税款：约 1.01 亿股随发行归属，员工要为此缴税。Anthropic 预扣其中 5160 万股，自掏约 38 亿美元代缴税款，免掉了上市时常见的被迫抛售。

他的收尾是：流通盘 7%，日历一整年，供给有计划、有日期，规模是发行量的好几倍。到 6 月之前，想离场的股东没法靠对冲离场，只能等，或者在每个解禁日卖掉当天释放的那部分。

附图是 ANT 上市首日的日内走势截图（编者据图读出：发行价 83 美元，盘中最高约 115 美元，截图时报 97.37 美元，较发行价上涨 17.31%）。TheValueist 说的“承压”指的是开盘之后的回落，并非跌破发行价。

跟踪 ANT 的读者，可以把他给的几个日期记下来：12 月 15 日第一次常规解禁，12 月中旬第一份卖空数据，期权上市后的看跌偏度，以及 2027 年 6 月 1 日对冲禁令解除。持有人在偷偷对冲这件事，是他基于激励结构的推断，帖中没有给出直接证据。

$ANT ANT IS HEAVY, AND SOME OF THE SHORTS ARE PROBABLY THE OWNERS

$ANT ANT IS HEAVY, AND SOME OF THE SHORTS ARE PROBABLY THE OWNERS

Anthropic debuted today into the weakest tape of the year for the AI complex, and the stock has been under pressure from the open. The easy read is that the market is rejecting the valuation. There is a mechanical explanation that fits better, and it starts with who owned this company before today.

Anthropic's cap table is not only institutions. Across years of private rounds, secondary sales and SPVs, a long tail of private individual investors holds pre-IPO stock, in many cases sitting on windfalls of ten to fifty times their money. As of this morning those windfalls are marked to market, and most of them cannot be sold. The lockup runs up to a full year.

A holder in that position has an obvious trade available: short the listed stock against the locked shares, or buy puts once options list, and the paper windfall becomes a locked gain whatever the next year brings. Every point the stock falls from here pays out on the hedge against shares the holder cannot sell until 2027.

Three frictions stand in the way, and they explain why this is pressure rather than a flood. The lockup bars hedging until day 195, June 1, 2027, and then allows only 20% of a holder's remaining restricted shares. A short against the box is a constructive sale for tax purposes, so the gain is taxed as if it were realized. And borrow in a float of about 7% is scarce, expensive and recallable.

But detection is not one of the frictions. Short interest is published twice a month as a single aggregate number with no names attached. No filing identifies a small holder's short. The company and the underwriters, the parties to the lockup, have no window into anyone's brokerage account. For thousands of small private holders, nobody would know.

So some of the early short interest in this name will not be bears betting against Anthropic. It will be owners insuring windfalls they are not allowed to sell. The footprints to watch are the first published short interest print in mid-December, the put skew once ANT options list, and the release dates below, starting December 15, which test how much eligible stock actually gets sold by holders who could not hedge.

The reason the incentive lasts is the schedule itself. Here it is in full.

THE LOCKUP THAT CREATES THE INCENTIVE

Anthropic priced at $83 with about 980M shares in public hands, roughly 7% of the 13.09B shares outstanding. Behind that float sit about 15.5B restricted shares, options and awards, and they do not free up on one date. The prospectus sets 23 release dates across a full year, keyed partly to fixed day counts and partly to earnings reports. Every figure below is an eligibility ceiling. The filing does not say any holder intends to sell.

THE TWO TRACKS

About 2.7B shares, held by employees, non-founder directors and officers, and a group of other stockholders, are restricted for up to 180 days, ending at the earlier of two trading days after Q1 2027 results or May 17, 2027.

About 8.0B shares, held by the seven co-founders and the larger stockholders above 0.5%, are restricted for up to 365 days, ending at the earlier of two trading days after Q3 2027 results or November 18, 2027.

THE RELEASE CALENDAR

Day one, November 19, 2026: up to 316M shares, the non-founder insiders' 10% allowance. The CFO and chief accounting officer are named in the filing as eligible to sell from the first session.

December 15, 2026 and January 5, 2027: up to 299M on each date. 914M cumulative.

January 19 and February 2, 2027: up to 349M on each date. 1.61B cumulative, about twice the shares sold in the offering as filed.

Second trading day after FY2026 results: up to 1.7B. 3.31B cumulative. This step carries the insiders' further 35% release.

13, 27, 41, 55 and 69 days after FY2026 results: up to 474M on each date. 5.68B cumulative.

Q1 2027 results or May 17, 2027, whichever comes first: up to 3.0B. 8.68B cumulative. The largest single release in the structure. The 180-day track ends here.

34, 48, 62 and 76 days after Q1 2027 results: up to 449M on each date. 10.48B cumulative.

Second trading day after Q2 2027 results: up to 562M. 11.04B cumulative.

27, 41, 55, 69 and 83 days after Q2 2027 results: up to 505M on each date. 13.57B cumulative.

Q3 2027 results or November 18, 2027, whichever comes first: the remaining 1.9B. 15.47B cumulative. Everything is free.

INSIDE THE 365-DAY TRACK

The percentages for a large holder run: 1.8% on each of days 27 and 48, 2.1% on each of days 62 and 76, 3.75% after FY2026 results, five releases of 2.85% after those results, 4.2% at the 180-day point for 30.0% cumulative, four releases of 5.6% after Q1 2027 results, 7.0% after Q2 2027 results, five releases of 6.3% after those results for 90.9% cumulative, and the balance at the end. A holder of 100M shares can sell 7.8M by February 2 and 30M by next May. The rest rides for a year.

THE SIDE DOORS

Hedging: barred until day 195, June 1, 2027, then up to 20% of remaining restricted shares for certain stockholders, about 719M shares in total. The underwriters may also release shares early at their discretion, and some releases trigger matching releases for other holders.

The $26B of Class N stock sold alongside the IPO to Google, Nvidia, AMD, Broadcom, Micron, Microsoft and TSMC Partners carries no lockup at all. It is restricted stock, generally not resalable by non-affiliates for six months.

About 101M shares vest with the offering and employees owe tax on them. Anthropic withholds 51.6M of those shares and pays about $3.8B of the tax itself, which removes the usual forced selling at listing.

The float is 7%. The calendar is a year. The supply is scheduled, dated, and larger than the offering many times over. And until June, the owners who want out cannot hedge their way out. They can only wait, or sell what each date frees.
