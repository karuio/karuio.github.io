中文改写

Tomasz Tunguz 在一篇 X Article 里提出：AI 需求已经快于供给，美国国内数据中心建设又在放缓，美国很快会消费比自己生产更多的 token。到那时，美国会变成 token 净进口国，就像它现在是稀土净进口国一样。

这是他的分析框架，不是已经发生的贸易统计。他写了一个基础模型，估计这个转折会出现在 2028 年到 2030 年之间，具体取决于有多少规划中的设施被取消。

按他非常粗略的估计，在新数据中心陆续上线的前提下，现有体系大约能承接每年 2.5 倍的 token 增长。超过这个速度后，芯片和模型效率的提升就无法抵消国内需求增长。

他给出了三档情景：如果 45% 的规划项目无法接入电网，进口会在 2028 年开始；把取消比例降到 20%，时间会推迟到 2029 年；即便所有提议中的设施都按时建成，缺口仍会在 2030 年出现。这些年份都来自他的模型，本站未复核其假设和计算。

他也指出，所有物理模型都假设算力价格保持不变。现实中，供给一紧，价格就会跳升。到目前为止，他认为需求对价格非常不敏感。过去两个季度，短期 GPU 租金从每小时 5 美元升到超过 13 美元；Anthropic 以溢价签下了数十亿美元的突发算力；总需求几乎没有眨眼，买家只是付了这笔过路费。GPU 租金和 Anthropic 订单金额是他文中的陈述。

他接着问，价格要高到什么程度，人们才会退缩。是那些烧掉资本去预训练下一代前沿模型的实验室？是每周烧掉数亿 token、用 vibe-coding 做定制应用的企业团队？还是被要求为自动代理订阅每月支付 40 美元的消费者？40 美元是他举例用的价格，不是已核实的市场均价。

他认为，肯定存在某个价格，会让需求放慢，现有数据中心容量也就够用了。可如果只靠稀缺定价来压需求，就否认了一个基本的市场事实：生产成本更低时，需求会高出几个数量级。如果美国不建设足够的电力和设施去满足这部分需求，token 就会在别处生产，然后再被进口进来。

自 GPT 3.5 以来几乎不受约束的增长，在他看来接下来会受到调节。真正起作用的，会是这个国家关于数据中心该建多少、建多大、用多少电、建在哪里的公共选择。他认为，这些即将到来的限制，会重新塑造创业生态。原文同时发布在 tomtunguz.com。文章里提到的图表，此次未能取得可引用的媒体地址。

When Does the US Become a Net Importer of Tokens?

With AI demand outpacing supply, & domestic data center construction slowing, soon the US will consume more tokens than it produces. At that point, the US will become a net importer of tokens, just like it is a net importer of rare earth metals. A basic model estimates this switch between 2028 & 2030, depending on how many planned facilities get cancelled. Very roughly, the current systems, with new data centers coming online, can manage 2.5x annual token growth. Above that rate, chip & model efficiency gains can no longer offset domestic demand growth:

If 45% of planned projects fail to connect, imports start by 2028. Cut cancellations to 20%, & the deadline slips to 2029. Even if every proposed facility gets built on time, the gap still opens by 2030.

Every physical model assumes the price of compute stays constant. In reality, when supply chokes, prices spike. So far, demand has proven remarkably price-inelastic. Over the past two quarters, short-term GPU rental prices surged from $5 to more than $13 per hour, Anthropic signed billions in burst compute at premium rates, & overall demand barely blinked. Buyers simply paid the toll. At what price do people balk? Is it the model labs burning through capital to pre-train the next frontier? The enterprises financing vibe-coding teams that burn hundreds of millions of tokens a week building custom apps? Or consumers asked to pay $40 a month for an autonomous agent subscription? Surely there is some price at which demand will slow & current data center capacity will suffice. But throttling demand through scarcity pricing denies the basic market reality : at a lower cost of production, demand would be orders of magnitude higher. If we do not build the domestic power and facilities to satisfy that demand, tokens will simply be produced elsewhere & imported. The uninhibited growth since GPT 3.5 will be modulated not by lack of access to data, nor algorithmic limits, nor GPU manufacturing speed, but by the broader values of the country. The forthcoming limits on the quantity, size, electrical consumption, & location of data centers will reshape Startupland. Originally published on tomtunguz.com.
