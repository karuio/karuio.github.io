TheValueist：Anthropic 上市后的指数纳入时间表，标普 500 之外都开了快车道

中文改写

一家市值约 1 万亿美元的公司上市，真正能交易的股票只有约 600 亿美元。指数公司该怎么处理？TheValueist 在 Anthropic 定价前夜写了一篇长帖，梳理 2026 年各家指数规则的改动，以及 Anthropic 的实际纳入日程。

他的概括是：2026 年，指数行业为超大型 IPO 潮重新改了一遍规则。SpaceX 6 月是第一个试点，Anthropic 是第二个走这套新机制的交易。除了标普 500，所有主要指数商现在都有以交易日计算的快速通道。标普 500 研究了同样的改动，正式征求了意见，最后拒绝了。

这笔交易：市值上万亿，流通盘只有约 6%

按他的描述，Anthropic 当晚定价，11 月 19 日周四在纳斯达克以代码 ANT 开始交易。发行 8.25 亿股 A 类股，价格区间 70 到 78 美元，总股本约 131 亿股，按区间计算市值约 9160 亿到 1.02 万亿美元，完全稀释后约 1.4 万亿美元，市场还在传定价会高于区间。

对指数来说，关键数字是流通盘，市值破万亿倒在其次：公开发行的股份只占约 6%。另有 260 亿美元以私募配售给战略股东。据 MarketWatch，Alphabet 和英伟达各约 100 亿美元，AMD 25 亿美元，博通、美光和微软各 10 亿美元，台积电 5 亿美元；Alphabet 最终持股约 13%。内部人锁定 180 天，分批提前解禁。创始人通过一股特殊股份控制 50.1% 的投票权，S-1 本身也提示，多类股结构可能让这只股票失去部分指数的纳入资格。

旧规则：IPO 要先“熟化”

过去，IPO 要先经过一段观察期。标普 500 要求交易满 12 个月，并通过盈利测试。纳斯达克 100 要求交易满三个完整日历月，上市当月不算，所以 11 月上市的公司最快也要等到 12 月的年度重组，通常要等到下一年。罗素一般在季度维护时纳入 IPO，通常是上市后两到五个月。

唯一的例外是 MSCI。自 2007 年起，MSCI 就有超大型 IPO 提前纳入规则：规模达到分段门槛的 1.8 倍，就可以在第十个交易日收盘时纳入。他写道，二十年来，这套体系的设计思路是先让股票找到自己的价位、熬过锁定期，然后才让被动资金被迫买入。

2026 年各家指数商改了什么

纳斯达克 100（2026 年 5 月 1 日生效）

他认为这是最大的改动。新的快速纳入（Fast Entry）规则规定：新上市公司在第七个交易日收盘时测试，如果全市值排进现有成分股前 40 名，纳斯达克在第十个交易日后宣布纳入，第十五个交易日后生效，同时不剔除任何成分股，指数可以暂时超过 100 只。

两项配套改动同样重要。一是 2024 年 6 月才引入的 10% 最低自由流通比例要求被彻底取消。二是针对低流通盘巨头改写了权重公式：权重按调整后市值计算，等于股价乘以“总股本”和“自由流通股数的三倍”两者中较小的那个。于是 6% 的流通盘按公司 18% 的股份计权。

纳斯达克自己的 FAQ 算过一个完全一样的假设案例：一家市值 1 万亿美元、流通 6% 的公司，纳入时权重约 1%，跟踪基金需要买入约 60 亿美元股票，相当于全部可流通股的约 10%。他说，Anthropic 就是这个假设案例本身。纳斯达克 100 没有盈利测试，日均成交额只需超过 500 万美元。

富时罗素美国指数（2026 年 5 月 26 日生效）

罗素新增了第五个交易日后的快速纳入：IPO 的可投资（经流通调整）市值超过罗素前 500 名的分界点即可。对 Anthropic 而言，公布的门槛是 179.42 亿美元可投资市值，600 亿美元的流通盘轻松超过。权重只按可投资市值计算，没有三倍乘数；IPO 上市时或上市后 12 个月内，公开流通的股份至少要达到 5%。罗素的重组现在改为每半年一次，分别在 6 月和 12 月，这一点影响后面的时间表。

富时全球股票指数系列

第五个交易日后快速纳入，同时对照全市值和可投资市值两个门槛（北美分别为 437.41 亿美元和 145.80 亿美元）。Anthropic 两项都满足。

MSCI

MSCI 没有改规则，认为 2007 年的框架已经够用。提前纳入要求全市值至少达到中期规模分段门槛的 1.8 倍，经流通调整市值至少达到该门槛一半的 1.8 倍，在第一或第二个交易日收盘时测量。走快速通道的 IPO 豁免常规的流通和流动性筛选。决定最迟在第三个交易日开盘前公布，在第十个交易日收盘时生效；如果那天离定期审议很近，就与审议对齐。

标普道琼斯

他认为标普的结果分成两层，而这个分化正是看点。对宽基指数（标普全市场、标普补充指数、道琼斯美国全市场），标普在 2026 年 6 月 8 日起实施快速通道：IPO 的可投资权重因子不低于 0.10，或者经流通调整市值不低于指数中第 100 大公司的 10%，即可纳入，提前五个工作日通知，没有盈利或观察期要求。

对标普 500、中盘 400 和小盘 600，标普做了正式征求意见（4 月 30 日开始，5 月 28 日结束），提议的正是纳斯达克那一套：把观察期缩短到六个月，对超大市值公司豁免盈利和流通比例要求。6 月 4 日，标普全盘否决了自己的提案。12 个月观察期保留，10% 最低流通比例保留，GAAP 盈利测试也保留：最近一个季度持续经营业务净利润为正，过去四个季度合计也为正。标普明确表示，不应仅凭市值给予例外。

他还提到，此后的报道（包括参议员沃伦 6 月的一封信）称 SpaceX 曾大力游说各家指数商修改规则；纳斯达克和罗素改了，标普没有。

CRSP 和晨星

全市场指数同样在约五个交易日后快速纳入，所以全市场基金会在 11 月底前持有 Anthropic。

Anthropic 的实际纳入日程

首个交易日是 11 月 19 日周四。按感恩节（11 月 26 日周四休市）前后的交易日计算：

  • **MSCI 最先。**决定必须在 11 月 23 日周一、第三个交易日开盘前公布。第十个交易日是 12 月 3 日周四，但这天紧挨 11 月指数审议，按 MSCI 的对齐规则，生效时间指向 11 月 30 日周一收盘，与 11 月审议一起。无论哪种情况，MSCI 的资金都会在前八个交易日内进场。
  • **CRSP 和晨星全市场指数：**大约五个交易日，即 11 月底前。
  • **罗素美国指数：**按五个交易日规则，本应在 11 月 25 日周三纳入；但富时罗素 11 月 13 日的通知称，因为撞上感恩节，Anthropic 改为并入 12 月的半年度重组：12 月 11 日周五收盘后纳入，12 月 14 日周一开盘生效。
  • **纳斯达克 100：**第七个交易日的测试在 11 月 30 日周一收盘，市值接近 1 万亿美元的 Anthropic 稳进前 40（门槛约 1000 亿美元）。第十五个交易日是 12 月 10 日周四，但纳斯达克打算把这次纳入并入 12 月中旬的年度重组和再平衡，避免两次交易，所以实际在 12 月 21 日周一开盘前生效，按三倍流通公式，权重约 1%。富时全球指数也在同一天上午纳入，12 月 18 日周五收盘后、12 月 21 日周一生效。
  • **标普 500：**没有。12 个月观察期意味着最早 2027 年 11 月，而真正卡住的是盈利门槛：Anthropic 按 GAAP 仍在亏损，需要连续四个季度合计盈利、最近一个季度也盈利，委员会才能考虑，之后还要看委员会的裁量。他估计现实中要到 2028 年。

相比之下，标普全市场指数的快速通道几天内就能纳入。于是，持有全市场基金的投资者几乎立刻拥有 Anthropic，标普 500 基金的持有人却一股都没有。他认为，这两类最常见基金之间的分化是新现象，直接来自 6 月标普的两层决定。

资金账：一个月内被动资金要买走约三分之一流通盘

Intropic 估计，所有纳入 Anthropic 的指数加起来，被动需求约 190 亿美元（估算，转引自 MarketWatch）。对照约 600 亿美元的公开流通盘，指数跟踪资金要在第一个月内买下约三分之一的可交易供给，分三个固定日期完成：11 月 30 日（MSCI）、12 月 14 日（罗素）、12 月 21 日（纳斯达克 100 和富时全球）。

他认为，这在上市后的交易里形成一股结构性的、对价格不敏感的买盘，还会与这次发行的配售情况相互作用：多数基金要么没分到，要么只分到订单的一小部分，所以二级市场上既有失望的主动资金，也有被迫买入的被动资金，追逐的是同一块 6% 的流通盘。

这也是此前“资金抽离”的镜像：他写道，实际上为配售提供资金的被动资金体系，到 12 月会成为 ANT 的定期买家；而纳斯达克 100 内部对应的卖出，按比例分摊到所有成分股，每只约减 1% 的权重，对英伟达这类股票（截至 11 月 13 日占 QQQ 6.52%）只是小幅减持，冲击不大。

SpaceX 的先例：快速纳入保证的是资金流

SpaceX 的先例是一个警示。SPCX 6 月 12 日上市，7 月 7 日开盘前进入纳斯达克 100，权重不到 1%，进入指数后的头一段时间跌了约 15%。他的判断是，快速纳入不保证上市后的股价表现，它保证的只是资金流；而当锁定期开始释放股份、季度再平衡重设权重时，流入 6% 流通盘的资金流就是一把双刃剑。

Anthropic 的分批解禁会持续到 2027 年上半年，流通盘随之扩大。罗素和 MSCI 用流通调整市值计权，每次流通盘扩大，下一次审议时 Anthropic 的权重都会机械地上升，从而在 2027 年年中之前形成一连串规模较小、事先公布的被动买盘，其中 2027 年 6 月的罗素重组最大。

他认为，真正的大事件仍是标普 500，因为最深的被动资金池在那里，而按现行规则，它在 2027 年底之前不会发生，而且只能在盈利之后。按 Anthropic 的文件，其 2026 年收入为 332 亿美元，仍有经营亏损。如果它的 GAAP 利润像收入那样出现拐点，纳入标普 500 会成为 2028 年的资金流故事，并且会提前一年被市场看到。

治理上的一个注脚

他最后提到，指数行业在 2026 年证明了自己愿意为规模改写规则，标普的拒绝如今在商业压力下成了少数派立场。如果标普委员会哪天重新审视 6 月的决定，Anthropic 和 SpaceX 就是逼它回答这个问题的两个名字；那里的规则一旦改变，将是这一轮周期里最大的单一被动资金流事件。

这篇帖子写于定价之前，文中的发行区间、市值和各指数生效日期都是作者当时的梳理和推算，Intropic 的 190 亿美元也是估算。关注 ANT 的读者，可以把 11 月 30 日、12 月 14 日、12 月 21 日这三个日期和 2027 年上半年的解禁节点放进日历，再对照各指数商的正式公告核对。

原文

ANTHROPIC INDEX INCLUSION: HOW THE RULES CHANGED IN 2026

$ANT $MU $SNDK $LITE ANTHROPIC INDEX INCLUSION: HOW THE RULES CHANGED IN 2026

2026 is the year the index industry rewired itself for the mega-IPO wave. SpaceX was the test case in June, and Anthropic is the second deal through the new machinery. Every major provider except the S&P 500 now has a fast lane measured in trading days. The S&P 500 looked at the same changes, formally consulted on them, and refused.

THE DEAL THE INDEXES HAVE TO DIGEST

Anthropic prices tonight and trades tomorrow, Thu 11/19, on Nasdaq as ANT. 825M Class A shares at a $70 to $78 range against roughly 13.1B total shares, so a market cap of about $916B to $1.02T at the range, roughly $1.4T fully diluted, with talk of pricing above the range. The critical number for index purposes is not the trillion. It is the float: only about 6% of the shares are in the public offering. Another $26B goes in a private placement to strategic holders (Alphabet and Nvidia about $10B each, AMD $2.5B, Broadcom, Micron and Microsoft $1B each, TSMC $500M, per MarketWatch), and Alphabet ends up owning about 13%. Insiders are locked up 180 days with staggered early releases. The founders control 50.1% of the votes through a single share, and the S-1 itself warns the multi-class structure may make the stock ineligible for some indices. So the index question is: what does each provider do with a trillion-dollar company where only about $60B of stock actually trades?

THE OLD WORLD

Historically an IPO seasoned. The S&P 500 required 12 months of trading plus a profitability test. The Nasdaq-100 required three full calendar months, not counting the listing month, so a November IPO waited for the December annual reconstitution at best and usually the following year. Russell added IPOs at quarterly maintenance, typically two to five months after listing. The one exception was MSCI, which has had an early-inclusion rule for very large IPOs since 2007: meet a size test at 1.8x the segment cutoff and you can enter at the close of the tenth trading day. For two decades the system was designed to let a stock find its level, survive its lockup, and only then become forced buying for passive money.

WHAT CHANGED IN 2026, PROVIDER BY PROVIDER

NASDAQ-100 (EFFECTIVE MAY 1, 2026)

This is the biggest change. A new Fast Entry rule: if a new listing's full market cap ranks in the top 40 of current constituents when tested at the close of its seventh trading day, Nasdaq announces the addition after the tenth trading day and adds it after the fifteenth, without removing anyone, so the index can temporarily hold more than 100 names. Two supporting changes matter as much as the headline. The 10% minimum free float, itself only introduced in June 2024, was eliminated entirely. And the weighting formula was rewritten for low-float giants: weight is based on a modified market cap equal to the price times the lesser of total shares outstanding or three times the free-float shares. A 6% float therefore gets weighted as if it were 18% of the company. Nasdaq's own FAQ worked the exact hypothetical: a $1T company floating 6% enters at about a 1% weight, and tracking funds must buy roughly $6B of stock, about 10% of the entire available float. Anthropic is that hypothetical with a ticker. There is no profitability test in the Nasdaq-100, and average daily traded value only needs to clear $5M.

FTSE RUSSELL US (EFFECTIVE MAY 26, 2026)

Russell added fast entry after the fifth trading day for IPOs whose investable (float-adjusted) market cap clears the Russell Top 500 breakpoint; for Anthropic the stated threshold is $17.942B of investable cap, which a $60B float clears easily. Weight is on investable cap only, no 3x multiplier, and the IPO must have at least 5% of shares public at listing or within 12 months. Russell's reconstitution is now semi-annual, June and December, which matters for the timing below.

FTSE GLOBAL EQUITY INDEX SERIES

Fast entry after five trading days against full-cap and investable-cap thresholds ($43.741B and $14.580B for North America). Anthropic clears both.

MSCI

No rule change; the 2007 framework was deemed sufficient. Early inclusion requires full market cap of at least 1.8x the interim size-segment cutoff and float-adjusted cap of at least 1.8x half the cutoff, measured at the close of the first or second trading day. Fast-track IPOs are exempt from the usual float and liquidity screens. The decision is announced no later than before the open of the third trading day, effective at the close of the tenth, unless that date falls near a scheduled review, in which case the addition is aligned with the review.

S&P DOW JONES

Two-tier outcome, and the split is the story. For the broad indexes (S&P Total Market, S&P Completion, Dow Jones U.S. Total Stock Market), S&P added a fast track effective June 8, 2026: an IPO qualifies with an investable weight factor of at least 0.10, or a float-adjusted cap of at least 10% of the 100th-largest company in the index, with five business days' notice and no profitability or seasoning requirement. For the S&P 500, MidCap 400 and SmallCap 600, S&P ran a formal consultation (opened April 30, closed May 28) proposing exactly what Nasdaq did: cut seasoning to six months and waive profitability and float minimums for megacaps. On June 4 it rejected its own proposal in full. The 12-month seasoning stays, the 10% float minimum stays, and the GAAP profitability test stays: positive net income from continuing operations in the most recent quarter and across the trailing four quarters combined. S&P said explicitly that exceptions should not be granted solely based on market capitalization. Reporting since (including a June letter from Senator Warren) says SpaceX lobbied the providers hard for these changes; Nasdaq and Russell moved, S&P did not.

CRSP AND MORNINGSTAR

Total-market indexes also fast-track at about five trading days, which is why total-market funds pick Anthropic up by the end of November.

ANTHROPIC'S ACTUAL INCLUSION CALENDAR

First trade Thu 11/19. Counting trading days around Thanksgiving (closed Thu 11/26):

• MSCI is first. The decision must be announced by Mon 11/23, before the open of the third trading day. The tenth trading day is Thu 12/3, but because that falls right against the November index review, MSCI's alignment rule points to effectiveness at the close of Mon 11/30, with the November review. Either way, MSCI money is in within the first eight trading days. • CRSP and Morningstar total market: roughly five trading days, so by the end of November. • Russell US: the five-day rule would have landed the addition on Wed 11/25, but FTSE Russell's November 13 notices said the Thanksgiving collision pushes Anthropic into the December semi-annual reconstitution instead: added after the close Fri 12/11, effective at the open Mon 12/14. • Nasdaq-100: the seventh-day test is the close of Mon 11/30, and at anything near a $1T cap Anthropic is comfortably top 40 (the cutoff is roughly $100B). The fifteenth trading day is Thu 12/10, but Nasdaq is folding the addition into its mid-December annual reconstitution and rebalancing rather than trading twice, so the effective entry is the December reconstitution, before the open Mon 12/21, at roughly a 1% weight on the 3x-float formula. FTSE Global lands the same morning, effective Mon 12/21 after the close Fri 12/18. • S&P 500: nothing. Twelve months of seasoning means November 2027 at the earliest, and the profitability gate is the binding constraint regardless: Anthropic is GAAP loss-making, so it needs four consecutive profitable quarters summed positive plus a positive latest quarter before the committee can even consider it, then committee discretion on top. Realistically 2028. The S&P Total Market fast track, by contrast, can hold it within days, so an investor in a total-market fund owns Anthropic almost immediately while an S&P 500 fund holder owns none of it. That divergence between the two most common fund types is new, and it is a direct product of the June split.

THE FLOW MATH AND WHY IT MATTERS FOR THE TAPE

Intropic estimates total passive demand across all the indexes adding Anthropic at roughly $19B (estimate, via MarketWatch). Set that against a public float of about $60B: index trackers need to own on the order of a third of the tradable supply within the first month, in three dated cliffs, 11/30 (MSCI), 12/14 (Russell) and 12/21 (Nasdaq-100 and FTSE Global). That is a structural, price-insensitive bid under the aftermarket, and it interacts with the allocation dynamics in the deal: most funds were zeroed or filled at a fraction of their orders, so the aftermarket has both disappointed active money and forced passive money chasing the same 6% float. It is also the mirror image of the funding drain: the passive complex that effectively funded allocations becomes a scheduled buyer of ANT through December, while the offsetting sale inside the Nasdaq-100 is spread pro rata across all constituents at about 1% of each weight, a trim, not a hit, to names like Nvidia (6.52% of QQQ as of 11/13).

The SpaceX precedent is the cautionary data point. SPCX listed June 12, entered the Nasdaq-100 before the open July 7 at a weight below 1%, and fell about 15% over its first stretch in the index. Fast inclusion does not guarantee aftermarket performance; it guarantees flow, and flow into a 6% float cuts both ways when the lockup calendar starts releasing shares and weights get reset at the quarterly rebalances. The staggered lockup releases through the first half of 2027 will raise Anthropic's float, and every float step-up mechanically raises its Russell and MSCI weights (which use float-adjusted cap) at the next review, creating a series of smaller, pre-announced passive bids into mid-2027, with the June 2027 Russell reconstitution the largest. The genuinely big event remains the S&P 500, because that is where the deepest passive pool sits, and on current rules it cannot happen before late 2027 and only after profitability. If Anthropic's GAAP numbers inflect the way its revenue has ($33.2B in 2026 per its filing, still with an operating loss), the S&P 500 add becomes the 2028 flow story, telegraphed a year in advance.

One governance footnote worth knowing: the index industry just spent 2026 proving it will rewrite rules for size, and S&P's refusal is now the outlier position under commercial pressure. If the S&P committee ever revisits the June decision, Anthropic and SpaceX are the two names that force the question, and a rule change there would be the single largest passive flow event of the cycle.
