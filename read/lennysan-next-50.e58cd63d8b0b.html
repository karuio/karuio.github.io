中文改写

Lenny Rachitsky 写道，加入一艘火箭船的最好时间，是在所有人都知道它之前。他为此发布了 The Next 50：一份他认为最值得关注、也最值得尝试加入的 50 家早期创业公司名单。

“最热门”是他和团队按自己的数据规则排出的结果，并不是独立的行业排名。他使用 Harmonic 的数据，审视每一家早期创业公司，并按人数增长、近期融资和投资人关注度排序，然后选出前 50 名。完整名单发布在 lennysjobs.com/next50。

名单计划每季度更新。他也欢迎大家反馈，还应该考虑哪些因素。

这和已有的 Lenny 100 是分开的。Lenny 100 追踪的是人才密度高、在做更宏大问题的知名公司，地址是 lennysjobs.com/lenny100。

他在跟帖里说明了目的：帮助读者判断该去哪里找工作。为了把范围限定在顶级早期公司，他们只看同时满足这些条件的创业公司：

  1. 足够早：2023 年或以后成立，处于种子轮或 A 轮，估值低于 10 亿美元。
  2. 刚融到钱：过去六个月有融资，累计融资至少 500 万美元。
  3. 有顶级投资人：资本结构里至少有一家领先的风险投资机构。
  4. 仍在增长：员工人数在 10 到 250 人之间，且过去 90 天人数上升。

本季度入选的 50 家公司，他在跟帖里按顺序点了名，从 A Security、Act、Aegis AI 一直到 Wafer 和 Watchtower Labs。具体名单以原帖和网站页面为准。

I’m excited to launch The Next 50

The best time to join a rocketship is before everyone knows about it.

I’m excited to launch The Next 50: the 50 hottest early-stage startups to keep an eye on (and try to join).

Using @harmonic_ai’s data, the juiciest startup dataset out there, we looked at every early-stage startup and ranked them on headcount growth, recent funding, and investor interest.

We then picked the top 50.

Here's the full list: https://www.lennysjobs.com/next50

We’ll update it quarterly, and I’d love your feedback on what other factors we should consider.

P.S. This is in addition to the Lenny 100, the top 100 well-known companies with the highest talent density, tackling the most ambitious problems: https://www.lennysjobs.com/lenny100

My goal here is to help you figure out where to go try to get a job.

To keep this focused on top early-stage startups, we only look at startups that are:

  1. Early: founded in 2023 or later, at Seed or Series A, and valued under $1B
  2. Freshly funded: raised in the last six months, with at least $5M raised in total
  3. Backed by top investors: at least one leading venture firm on the cap table
  4. Growing: 10-250 employees, and headcount up over the last 90 days
