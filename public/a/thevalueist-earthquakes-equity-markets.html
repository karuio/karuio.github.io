TheValueist 用地震学读股市：11 月 20 日是主震还是前震，看 VIX 能否收破 35.89

他梳理 25 年经济物理学研究：幂律、余震衰减和自激发站得住，提前预测崩盘的记录站不住。结合 2006 年以来 11 次 VIX 35 事件的回测，他的基本情景是数周到数月高波动、6 到 12 个月向上；VIX 收盘超过 35.89 则改判为前震。

中文改写

11 月 20 日周五，VIX 收在 35.89。那天英伟达的收盘价比 10 月 6 日的收盘高点 239.24 美元低 47.5%，美光比 6 月 25 日的收盘高点 1213.56 美元低 51%。

TheValueist 想回答的是：这次冲击是主震，还是一场更大地震的前震？

他借用的工具来自地震学。他写道，股市和地震的类比在学术文献里有一个正式的研究方向，从 1990 年代末开始，叫经济物理学（econophysics），直接拿地震学的经验定律去检验市场数据。帖子开头附了 Atlas Peak Research 的 Peak-VIX 回测页面，文中把这项回测称作“我们自己的研究”。

三条被搬到市场上的地震定律

这个研究方向的主张很具体：市场和断层系统一样，缓慢积累应力，突然释放，之后按可测量的模式松弛。

被拿来检验的有三条：描述震级分布的古登堡-里克特定律（Gutenberg-Richter），描述余震衰减的大森定律（Omori），以及把主震和后续序列联系起来的生产力定律与 Bath 定律。

余震确实存在

  • Lillo 和 Mantegna（《Physical Review E》，2003）统计了 1987 年股灾后 100 个交易日里，标普 500 收益率超过某个阈值的次数。次数按幂律衰减，和地震余震的大森定律函数形式相同。
  • 这个结果无法用标准随机波动率模型解释。一项配套研究显示，GARCH(1,1) 模型完全描述不了崩盘后的指数走势。崩盘后的松弛没有典型时间尺度。
  • Petersen、Wang、Havlin 和 Stanley（《Physical Review E》，2010）做了大规模检验：2001 至 2002 年，531 只美股，一分钟级数据，219 次市场冲击。波动率冲击完全符合大森定律、生产力定律和 Bath 定律，主震的规模能预示前震和余震序列的形态。

前震：至今没人解决的问题

地震学没法实时区分前震和主震，市场也不行。第一次波动率飙升，有时就是事件本身，有时是更大事件的预警。

他用自家对 2006 年以来每一次已完结 VIX 35 事件的研究说明这一点：11 次里有 7 次，VIX 的收盘峰值就出现在信号当天；另外 4 次峰值来得更晚，而且这 4 次最后 VIX 都到了 45 或更高。

两类情况对应完全不同的交易。峰值出现在信号当天的，SPY 之后 21 个交易日的中位数回报是 +7.2%，252 个交易日是 +23.7%。峰值更晚出现的，SPY 在头 21 个交易日的中位数回报是 -9.2%。

震级服从幂律

  • Negrea（《Physica A》，2014）用成交量和指数收益构造了金融版的古登堡-里克特关系，以及类似里氏震级的“危机震级”。Selcuk 在 2004 年的《Financial earthquakes, aftershocks and scaling in emerging stock markets》里，早十年就用过同一套说法。
  • 幂律意味着大波动很少见，但绝不像正态分布暗示的那么少见。他写道，建立在高斯尾部上的风险模型，会“按时”被吓一跳。

多数冲击是市场自己引发的

  • 地震学用自激发点过程描述地震：每一次震动都会提高下一次的概率。Gresnigt、Kole 和 Franses（《Journal of Banking & Finance》，2015）就用这套方法搭了一个崩盘预警系统，在涵盖金融危机的标普 500 数据上，相对基于波动率的基准取得了正的 Hanssen-Kuiper 技能分数。
  • Filimonov 和 Sornette（《Physical Review E》，2012）用同样的 Hawkes 过程框架追问：市场里有多少变动来自新闻，有多少是市场自己触发自己？在 E-mini 标普 500 期货里，可归因于外部新闻的价格变动占比，从 1998 年的约 70% 降到 2007 年以来的不到 30%。用他的比喻，断层越来越多地自己蓄力、自己滑动。

临界点学派和它的批评者

  • Johansen、Ledoit 和 Sornette 认为崩盘是临界点：交易者之间的模仿慢慢累积，把系统推向不稳定，过程中伴随对数周期振荡。他们在 1929 到 1998 年的 8 次崩盘里都记录到了这种特征。其中两位作者是在 UCLA 地球物理与行星物理研究所做的这项研究，从地震学借来的方法是原样照搬。
  • Sornette 的统计检验发现，纳斯达克和道指最大的 1% 到 10% 回撤，和其余样本属于显著不同的分布。这个结果发展成 2009 年的“龙王”（dragon-king）假说：无论是金融回撤还是地震能量，最大的那些事件和幂律主体的生成机制不同，而且可能有可观测的前兆。
  • 批评就来自这批文献内部，而且打中了要害。Feigenbaum 发现，把崩盘前最后一年的数据去掉，1987 年的对数周期拟合就失去统计显著性。Sornette 的回应是，丢掉最接近临界点的数据，等于把现象本身丢掉了。Hawkes 临界性估计也经历过同样的争论：有研究显示，体制切换带来的校准偏差，会在不存在临界性的地方制造出表面上的临界性。
  • 他给出的 25 年后的公允结论是：**描述性定律（幂律震级、大森衰减、自激发）在实证金融里属于最扎实的一类；但这批文献里没有任何前瞻性的、样本外的崩盘择时记录经得起检验。**站得住的，是冲击发生之后的条件统计。

平静期是蓄力期

Andersen 等人（《PLoS ONE》，2011）把全球各交易所建模成用弹簧连在一起的滑块，直接对应描述板块运动的 Burridge-Knopoff 黏滑模型，并复现了应力的积累和突然释放，也就是在各交易所之间连锁传导的“价格地震”。

在他看来，这就是上面所有现象背后的机制：杠杆、被压缩的价差、做空波动率和拥挤仓位，都是在积累应力。平静的时期是蓄力阶段，并不安全。

市场眼下走到哪一步

  • 这一轮序列从 11 月 20 日周五 VIX 收在 35.89 开始。
  • 目前 VIX 的收盘峰值仍是 35.89，所以 11 月 20 日暂时被归为主震。在全部 11 次已完结事件里，SPY 从信号收盘起 21 个交易日的中位数回报是 +6.5%，从峰值起 252 个交易日是 +27.7%。
  • 余震序列还没结束。周三 VIX 盘中到过 33.47，VVIX 当天涨 13.43 点；冲击一周后 VIX 期限结构仍然倒挂，周三下午 VIX 为 28.81，三个月 VIX 为 26.77。
  • CNN 恐惧与贪婪指数 11 月 24 日收在 10.96，是 10 月 16 日开始的这段行情里的极值。Zweig 广度冲力指标周二为 0.450，要在 12 月 3 日收盘前达到 0.615 才算完成。
  • 仓位已经部分出清。摩根士丹利主经纪商数据显示，美国多空基金的净敞口是 41%，一年来最低，处于 2010 年以来的第 6 百分位。但资金流估算显示，1169 亿美元的半导体仓位累积只平掉了 36%，上一轮周期平掉了 60%；另外估计有 410 亿到 470 亿美元的杠杆 ETF 机械再平衡买盘，让周一的收盘显得更好看。
  • 剩下的压力集中在 AI 信贷。甲骨文五年期 CDS 在 11 月 24 日报 362.80，为五年来最高；CoreWeave 的信用互换隐含五年违约概率超过 70%（ICE Data Services，经彭博，2026 年 11 月 25 日）。

他对后市的预期

  • 基本情景：不会走出 V 形。大森衰减没有特征时间尺度，所以他认为实事求是的预期是：在 6 到 12 个月向上的路径里，夹着数周到数月的高波动和剧烈的余震交易日。中位数为正，路径很颠簸。
  • 确认信号：VIX 期限结构重新穿过平价、结束倒挂，广度冲力在 12 月 3 日前完成，VIX 回落穿过 25 且不再创收盘新高。
  • 尾部情景是同步化，也就是龙王模式下几条断层一起破裂：AI 信贷，加上 12 月的政策日程（美联储 12 月 8 日至 9 日开会，英伟达的 10-Q 12 月 4 日到期，12 月 18 日三重到期日之后资金面的支撑变薄）。他认为，如果出现第二段下跌，最可能来自信贷和 AI 板块一起出问题，而非新的外部冲击。

一个数字决定分类

VIX 只要收盘高于 35.89，11 月 20 日就从主震改判为前震；而峰值更晚出现的那一类，历史上从没有到不了 45 的。

在这个数字出现之前，他认为历史记录既不支持在余震里降低风险敞口，也不支持在序列明朗之前加仓。

按他的框架，接下来可以盯住几个能核对的指标：VIX 收盘是否突破 35.89，12 月 3 日前广度冲力能否完成，VIX 期限结构是否结束倒挂，以及甲骨文和 CoreWeave 的信用指标。他的分类依据是 11 次已完结的 VIX 35 事件和自家回测，样本不大，属于条件统计，不能当作价格预测。

EARTHQUAKES AND EQUITY MARKETS: WHAT 25 YEARS OF RESEARCH ACTUALLY FOUND

https://t.co/Er6J13w6lQ

EARTHQUAKES AND EQUITY MARKETS: WHAT 25 YEARS OF RESEARCH ACTUALLY FOUND

THE PREMISE

• The comparison between equity markets and earthquakes is not a metaphor in the academic literature. It is a research program, running since the late 1990s under the name econophysics, in which seismology's empirical laws are tested directly against market data. • The claim is specific: markets, like fault systems, load stress slowly, release it suddenly, and relax afterward in patterns that can be measured. • Three of seismology's laws have been carried over and tested: the Gutenberg-Richter law for magnitudes, the Omori law for aftershocks, and the productivity and Bath laws linking a main shock to its sequence.

AFTERSHOCKS ARE REAL

• Lillo and Mantegna (Physical Review E, 2003) counted how often the S&P 500 return exceeded a threshold in the 100 trading days after the 1987 crash. The count decayed as a power law, the same functional form as the Omori law for earthquake aftershocks. • Their result could not be explained by standard stochastic volatility models, and a companion study showed a GARCH(1,1) model cannot describe post-crash index dynamics at all. The relaxation after a crash has no typical timescale. • Petersen, Wang, Havlin and Stanley (Physical Review E, 2010) ran the test at scale: 219 market shocks across 531 US stocks at one-minute resolution over 2001 and 2002. The Omori, productivity and Bath laws all held for volatility shocks, with the size of the main shock predicting the shape of both the preshock and aftershock sequences.

FORESHOCKS: THE PROBLEM NOBODY HAS SOLVED

• Seismology cannot tell a foreshock from a mainshock in real time. Neither can markets. A first volatility spike is sometimes the event itself and sometimes the warning of a larger one. • Our own study of every completed VIX 35 episode since 2006 makes the point with prices. In 7 of 11 episodes the VIX printed its closing peak on the signal day itself. In the other 4 the peak came later, and all four eventually printed a VIX of 45 or higher. • The two classes are different trades. When the peak came on the signal day, SPY returned a median +7.2% in 21 sessions and +23.7% in 252 sessions. When the peak came later, SPY returned a median -9.2% in the first 21 sessions.

MAGNITUDES FOLLOW A POWER LAW

• Negrea (Physica A, 2014) built a financial Gutenberg-Richter relation and a Richter-style crisis magnitude out of trading volume and index returns. Selcuk's "Financial earthquakes, aftershocks and scaling in emerging stock markets" (2004) applied the same vocabulary a decade earlier. • The consequence of a power law is that large moves are rare but never as rare as a normal distribution implies. Risk models built on Gaussian tails will be surprised on schedule.

MOST SHOCKS ARE SELF-INFLICTED

• The seismology of earthquakes runs on self-exciting point processes: each shock raises the probability of the next. Gresnigt, Kole and Franses (Journal of Banking & Finance, 2015) built a crash early warning system on exactly that machinery, and on S&P 500 data through the financial crisis it produced positive Hanssen-Kuiper skill scores against volatility-based benchmarks. • Filimonov and Sornette (Physical Review E, 2012) used the same Hawkes process framework to ask how much of the market is news and how much is the market triggering itself. In E-mini S&P 500 futures, the share of price changes attributable to outside news fell from about 70% in 1998 to under 30% since 2007. The fault increasingly loads and slips on its own.

THE CRITICAL POINT SCHOOL, AND ITS CRITICS

• Johansen, Ledoit and Sornette argued that crashes are critical points: a slow build-up of imitation among traders drives the system toward instability, decorated by log-periodic oscillations. They documented the signature across 8 crashes from 1929 to 1998. Two of the authors did this work at UCLA's Institute of Geophysics and Planetary Physics. The transfer from seismology was literal. • Sornette's statistical tests found the largest 1% to 10% of drawdowns in the Nasdaq and the Dow belong to a distribution significantly different from the rest of the population. That result became the dragon-king hypothesis (2009): the very largest events are generated by a different mechanism than the power-law body, in financial drawdowns and in earthquake energies alike, and may carry observable precursors. • The critics are inside the literature, and they land real blows. Feigenbaum showed the 1987 log-periodic fit loses statistical significance when the final year of data before the crash is removed. Sornette's answer is that discarding the data nearest a critical point discards the phenomenon itself. The Hawkes criticality estimates have been through the same fight, with calibration biases from regime shifts shown to manufacture apparent criticality where none exists. • The fair verdict after 25 years: the descriptive laws (power-law magnitudes, Omori decay, self-excitation) are as well established as anything in empirical finance. No prospective, out-of-sample crash timing record from this literature survives scrutiny. What survives is the conditional statistics after a shock.

STRESS BUILD-UP AND RELEASE

• Andersen and co-authors (PLoS ONE, 2011) modeled the world's stock exchanges as blocks linked by springs, a direct correspondence to the Burridge-Knopoff stick-slip models of tectonic plates, and recovered the build-up and sudden release of stress that generates cascading price-quakes across exchanges. • That is the mechanism under everything above: leverage, compressed spreads, short volatility and crowded positioning are stress loading. The quiet periods are the loading phase, not the safe phase.

WHERE THE MARKET STANDS NOW

• The current sequence began on Friday, November 20, 2026, when the VIX closed at 35.89. NVIDIA closed that day down 47.5% from its October 6 closing high of 239.24, and Micron down 51% from its June 25 closing high of 1,213.56. • The closing VIX peak is still 35.89, which provisionally classifies November 20 as the mainshock. Across all 11 completed episodes, SPY returned a median +6.5% in the 21 sessions from the signal close and +27.7% in the 252 sessions from the peak. • The aftershock sequence is not finished. On Wednesday the VIX reached 33.47 intraday with VVIX up 13.43 points on the day, and the VIX curve remains inverted a week after the shock, at 28.81 against a three-month VIX of 26.77 on Wednesday afternoon. • CNN Fear and Greed closed at 10.96 on November 24, the extreme of an episode that began on October 16. The Zweig breadth thrust measure stood at 0.450 on Tuesday and needs 0.615 by the December 3 close to complete. • Positioning has partly cleared. Morgan Stanley prime brokerage puts US long/short net exposure at 41%, a one-year low and the 6th percentile since 2010. Against that, flow estimates put the unwind at only 36% of a $116.9B semi positioning build, against 60% unwound in the prior cycle, and an estimated $41B to $47B of mechanical levered-ETF rebalancing buying flattered Monday's close. • The stress that remains is concentrated in AI credit. Oracle five-year CDS printed 362.80 on November 24, a five-year high, and CoreWeave credit swaps imply a greater than 70% five-year default probability (ICE Data Services, via Bloomberg, November 25, 2026).

WHAT I EXPECT GOING FORWARD

• Base case: not a V. Omori decay has no characteristic timescale, so the honest expectation is weeks to months of elevated volatility and sharp aftershock days inside a rising 6 to 12 month path. Positive medians, violent path. • Confirmation would come from the term structure flipping back through parity, the breadth thrust completing by December 3, and the VIX decaying through 25 without a new closing high. • The tail case is synchronization, the dragon-king pattern in which separate faults rupture together: AI credit, the December policy calendar (the FOMC meets December 8 and 9, NVIDIA's 10-Q is due December 4, and flow support thins after the December 18 triple witching). If a second leg comes, it most likely comes from credit and the AI complex going together, not from a new outside shock. • One number decides the classification. A VIX close above 35.89 turns November 20 into a foreshock, and the later-peak class has never failed to reach 45. Until that print, the record argues against de-risking into the aftershocks, and against adding before the sequence declares itself.
